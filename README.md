Vulkan - A weird horror themed - zombie shooting game.

you're driving a bike: searching for HEAVEN but you suddenly feel something and fall down. and when u wake up, you see a random portal appear in front of you. you enter the portal and uh fight cuz you're angry you weren't sent to heaven :c

CONTROLS:
1. WASD - for movement
2. ] - to skip cutscene
3. left click to shoot
4. shift to crouch
5. Lcontrol to sprint
6. ESC to pause.
